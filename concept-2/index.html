01—Our thinking

Why we exist

Influence isn't measured by the size of an audience. It starts with the relationship a creator has with it.
  • i.

    Relevance

    The right audience matters more than the biggest one.

  • ii.

    Trust

    Built slowly by creators. Protected carefully by us.

  • iii.

    Creativity

    Room for creators to do what they do best.

  • iv.

    Connection

    Partnerships that feel natural on both sides of the screen.

02—What we do

We sit between brands and the creators they should know — and look after everything that happens next.

  1. (a)

    Discovery & matching

    We identify creators whose audience, voice and values align with what a brand stands for — and brands worth a creator's name.

  2. (b)

    Partnership management

    Outreach, briefs, agreements, timelines and approvals — handled with care and communicated clearly to both sides.

  3. (c)

    Creative direction

    We help shape ideas that honour the brand and feel entirely at home in the creator's world.

  4. (d)

    Lasting relationships

    The best partnerships aren't one-off posts. We help the right ones grow into something that lasts.

04—Our approach

We'd rather make one right introduction than ten convenient ones.

  1. 01

    Fit before following

    Audience alignment comes first. Size is a detail, not the decision.

  2. 02

    The creator's voice stays theirs

    Audiences can tell when content is scripted. We brief, we don't dictate.

  3. 03

    Clarity for everyone

    Expectations, terms and timelines agreed up front — no surprises later.

  4. 04

    Long-term over one-off

    Repeat partnerships build credibility that a single post never can.

05—How partnerships work

A considered process, from first conversation to finished work.

  1. i.

    Listen

    We learn what a brand is building, or what a creator makes and who it's for.

  2. ii.

    Match

    We propose partnerships where audience, content and values genuinely align.

  3. iii.

    Introduce

    Both sides meet with a shared brief, clear terms and agreed timelines.

  4. iv.

    Collaborate

    We manage the day-to-day so the creator can create and the brand can trust it.

  5. v.

    Reflect

    We review what resonated, and shape what comes next together.
